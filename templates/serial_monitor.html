LCR-II / LCR-600 (RS-232 via J3):
  9600 baud 8N1 no flow control
  J3 pin48(TXD/orange) → USB-RS232 RX
  J3 pin49(RXD/gray) → USB-RS232 TX
  J3 pin51(GND/white) → GND
  Jumper J10 on CPU board must be in RS-232 position

LCR.iQ (CAN via J8 — CAN-to-RS232 bridge):
  J8 pin46 = CAN-H → bridge CAN-H
  J8 pin45 = CAN-L → bridge CAN-L
  J8 pin43 = EARTH/Shield → bridge shield/GND
  Bridge serial side → your USB-RS232 converter
  SAE J1939 @ 250 kbps CAN, 115200 baud serial side
