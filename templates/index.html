LectroCount Register Simulator

A working model of three Liquid Controls LectroCount register generations — operator interface, k-Factor calibration, VCF temperature compensation, and the pulser/RTD fault-diagnosis paths — built from the engineering manuals. Pick a meter below; each runs as its own independent instance, so calibrating the LCR-II doesn't touch the LCR 600's state.

LectroCount LCR‑II

Rotary selector switch, two-button (SELECT / INCREASE) menu navigation, 4 product calibrations. The simplest of the three — closest to the original Lap Pad-driven design.

10,000 Hz pulse max · 4.5A · 6-char LCD

LectroCount LCR 600

Full alphanumeric keypad, 5.7" color screen, and the Point-of-Sale layer: tax categories, cash discounting, priced tickets. 16 product calibrations.

10,000 Hz pulse max · 4.5A · 5.7" QVGA LCD

LCR.iQ

The current CENTRILOGiQ platform: 7" touchscreen-style UI, Bluetooth/Wi-Fi, the SENSEiQ 4-20mA expansion board for tank inventory, and continuously variable digital valve control.

7500 Hz pulse max · 5A · 7" 800x480 TFT
